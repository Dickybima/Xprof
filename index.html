How it works

01

Paste a handle

Drop any X username. We pull the name, avatar and bio and prefill the coin — ticker included. Everything stays editable.

02

Launch on a curve

Your wallet signs one transaction on Robinhood Chain. The full supply goes into a fair bonding curve priced in ETH.

03

Graduate

When the curve fills, it graduates into a permanently locked Uniswap V4 pool. Creator fees flow to the launcher's wallet from the first trade.

FAQ

Who earns the creator fees?

The wallet that launches the coin. Creator fees from every trade on the bonding curve, and later on the graduated pool, go straight to the launcher's wallet.

Can I change the coin's name?

No. The coin name, ticker, avatar and X link are taken from the X profile and locked, so a profile coin always matches the account it represents.

Is this financial advice?

No. Tokens of X profiles are highly speculative and are not endorsed by the people they reference unless verified.
